Unlimited people is not unlimited usage

A fair pricing model distinguishes shared participation, specialist acceleration and supplier resources consumed.

8 minute read19 July 2026

Per-seat pricing is understandable, but it creates the wrong incentive for shared customer work. Delivery, finance and leadership are excluded because every person becomes a recurring cost. The company then pays for incomplete visibility.

The workspace creates shared value

A Business workspace funds CRM, inbox, projects, tasks, approvals, reporting, API and governance. It can reasonably include unlimited ordinary collaborators because more responsible people participating usually improves the system.

Specialist tools create concentrated value

Bulk campaign execution and power calling provide direct acceleration to named operators. A Campaign or Calling licence charges the people receiving that capability without taxing colleagues who only approve, deliver or report.

Supplier usage creates variable cost

Telephone minutes, rented numbers, purchased data, model processing, storage and exceptional delivery volume produce external costs. Calling all of this “unlimited” either creates surprise restrictions or forces careful customers to subsidise heavy consumption.

Unlimited collaboration can be honest. Unlimited externally supplied resources usually cannot.

What the bill should show

  1. Workspace: one predictable company price.
  2. Licences: named Campaign and Calling operators.
  3. Usage: visible consumption with estimates, budgets and hard caps.

This lets finance forecast the software while operations control variable spend.
