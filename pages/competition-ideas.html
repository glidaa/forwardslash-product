Aircall · homepage

1. Hero

They say: “Customer communications, evolved…” + rotating AI: handles end to end · qualifies/resolves · coaches in real time · always-on · automates workflows.

CTAs: TRY FOR FREE · TALK TO SALES

Why it works: Category lift (“communications”) + evolution promise + AI as the mechanism. Two doors: free vs sales. No price in the hero.

We use: Same shape — category + outcome + free/talk. Not their “customer communications” (support/phone). Ours is outbound.

Ours could look like: “B2B outreach, from your mailbox.” · “One book from first list to booked meeting.” · Start free · Talk to us

2. Platform line

They say: “Aircall is the intuitive, AI-powered platform bringing together intelligent voice agents, automated workflows, & adaptive real-time coaching at scale.”

Why it works: Names three pillars in one breath so the page can expand each.

Ours could look like: “Forwardslash is the quiet outbound system: lists, own-mailbox send, and calling — one book to grow the campaign.”

3. Nested pillars (don’t flatten these)

They say — Team-led: “Team-led customer conversations powered by a modern phone system.” Children: Clear and reliable calling · One collaborative workspace · Connected across every channel.

They say — AI-assisted: “AI Assistants that support your team in real time.” Children: Live coaching during calls · Live transcription and insights · Automated follow-ups and CRM updates.

They say — AI-led: “AI Agents that provide 24/7 customer coverage.” Children: Autonomous resolution · Always-on availability · Escalation with full context.

Why it works: Parent = job. Children = explained features. Buyer can skim parents or dig. AI-assisted isn’t one blob — three concrete proves.

Ours could look like — three parents:

  • Start the campaign — Load a list · Send from your mailbox · Dial from your number
  • Work the replies — Shared contact history · Booking page · Tasks on the person
  • Grow the book — Sequences · Approval before send · Seats when the firm is bigger

4. Product overview lines

They say: “Better customer conversations happen here” · “AI-powered customer communications and intelligence” · “Aircall unifies your business’ voice and digital channels into one seamless platform, bringing all your customer conversation data together into one view.” · CTA Get → get-started

Why it works: Unify + one view = benefit. “Intelligence” sells AI without a feature dump in the H1.

Ours could look like: “B2B outreach happens here.” · “Your mailbox, your phone, one campaign book.” · Get started / Start free

5. Site chrome (steal the map)

They have: Solutions (top uses + by industry) · Partners / affiliate · Resources & docs · Legal center (T&Cs, privacy, DPA, subprocessors, telecom).

Why it works: Makes a phone vendor feel enterprise-safe and partner-ready.

We use: Same nav buckets when we go public — not every legal doc on day one, but the checklist exists.

5d. Product overview — “Can you relate?” pains

  • We need to hit high volume targets on customer calls and texts.
  • Our team needs an easier way to collaborate efficiently.
  • We need a simpler way to personalize our customer conversations.
  • We want to track our communications better to improve the customer experience.

Ours could look like (outbound pains): Hit outreach volume without a send farm · Keep the list and replies in one book · Personalize from our mailbox · See who opened / answered / booked

Communicate with confidence — Stop using personal numbers · Fluid calls and texts · Quality controls · Mute / hold / transfer. Links: International Numbers · Business Text · Call Recordings · Mobile apps.

Keep customers at the heart — Less repetition · Context when you need it · Auto-log to CRM · Less tab jumping. Links: Shared Inbox · IVR · Business Hours · Tags.

Turn conversations into intelligence — Real-time metrics · Inbound/outbound reports · Avoid surprises · Call timeline. Links: Live Monitoring · Analytics · Analytics+ · Call Timeline.

More productivity · powered by AI — Power dial · AI context snapshots · AI summaries · Trending topics. Links: Power Dialer · Voicemail Drop · AI Call Summaries · AI Trending Topics.

Trust: 23,000+ companies · SOC2 · GDPR · HIPAA. Integrations: 250+ (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk…).

Ours could look like — four clusters: Own your channels · Work the person · See the campaign · Agents on the book

CloudTalk · homepage

6. Category + hero

They say: “AI business phone system for sales and support”

They say: “Every call answered, 24/7. By your team, or by AI.”

They say: “Smart routing, outbound dialers and AI voice agents — on the same queues, the same integrations, the same local numbers in 160+ countries.”

CTAs: Book a Demo · Start Free Trial

Concrete free: “Free 50 AI minutes · 14-day Trial · No Card Required” · “Hear me” / get a call from the AI · “Rings you in 10 seconds”

Why it works: Category is instant. Promise is binary and vivid (answered / team or AI). Support line stacks proof nouns. Free offer is felt — minutes + a real call — not abstract “start free.”

Ours could look like:

  • Category: “Outbound CRM for founders who email and call.”
  • Promise: “First list live in ten minutes. From your mailbox.”
  • Support: “Lists, own-channel send, dialling, booking — one book, AU / UK / IE.”
  • Concrete free: “Load 50 contacts free · no signup · no card” or “Publish a booking link in ten minutes.”
  • CTAs: Start free · Talk to us

7. “One phone system. Two kinds of agent.”

They say: team + AI on same queues / numbers; cover after-hours, overflow, first-touch qualification, follow-ups, FAQs.

Why it works: Dual agent story sells AI without replacing the human pitch.

Ours (different wedge): not AI receptionist. Dual story for us = Free job now / polish later or Solo human / Essentials team — same book, two depths.

HubSpot · smart CRM

8. The two-beat hang

They say: “Growing a business is hard. HubSpot makes it easier.”

Why it works: Empathy first, then relief. No features. Feels like a peer. Easy to remember and put on a billboard.

We use: Same two beats. Name our hard thing (outreach / pipeline / clients), then Forwardslash makes it easier.

Ours — candidates:

  • Managing clients is hard. Forwardslash makes it easier.
  • Outreach is hard. Forwardslash makes it easier.
  • Building a pipeline is hard. Forwardslash makes it easier.
  • Chasing B2B is hard. Forwardslash makes it easier.

9. Free CRM / Sales Hub (exact)

They say (CRM): “Free CRM Software for Startups & Small Businesses” · “Your data deserves better than spreadsheets.” · “100% free with no expiration date.” · “Get free CRM”

They say (Sales): “AI-powered tools to build pipelines and close deals.” · “Transform Every Rep Into a Top Performer with AI” · CTAs: Get a demo · Start 14-day free trial

Why it works: Free forever (not a trial) for CRM; Sales stacks AI + pipeline. “Smart CRM” = unify data + AI that knows when to reach out.

Ours: Don’t copy HubSpot’s whole CRM. Steal free foothold clarity + two-beat hang. Our free = one outbound job (list / booking), not “unlimited CRM forever.”
