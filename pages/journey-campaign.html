Click path

As a user — click path

  1. I open Campaigns (empty) → I click New campaign → a blank campaign opens.
  2. I upload a CSV or pick an existing list → contacts load into the audience side; merge fields appear.
  3. I work one rail: audience left, message right → I insert {{firstName}} (and other fields) in the draft; preview shows the row.
  4. I send from a connected mailbox — or send one test to myself → the campaign queues; status moves toward sent.
  5. I open Inbox → a reply arrives on the contact; I answer in the thread without leaving the book.
  6. Optional: from the contact I open a booking link or start a call → meeting or dial lands on the same person.

How competitors do this step

Plain read of list → draft → send → replies. Sources: competition-features (Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist) plus known Clay / Customer.io flows from design refs. Not our product letter.

Google / Microsoft / SMTP senders; lemwarm + Deliverability Hub; schedule, auto-launch/stop, inbox rotation. Unified inbox for multichannel replies; shared campaigns / review on team plans.
Clay Research table: find people, enrich columns, filter the sheet (Mobbin list-building refs). Not a mail composer — enrichment + formulas; copy lives downstream or via integrations. Export / push to Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist, CRM, Zapier-class tools — Clay does not own the send. No native reply inbox; replies stay in the outreach tool you sent from.
Customer.io People table with filter chips / segments (Mobbin People screen). Campaign / broadcast draft; delivery queue; Approve / Change review on email preview (Mobbin approval flows). Lifecycle / journey sends to known people — triggered and broadcast, not cold-farm volume. Replies and deliveries tracked in their messaging stack; review rounds before go-live, not a cold Unibox.

We refuse Instantly/Smartlead send-farm and DFY-mailbox identity. We keep own-mailbox send + one book. Clay is list/enrich only. Customer.io is lifecycle approval — useful for review rails, not our cold-outbound category.

Four frames only. Surfaces: campaigns + inbox.

Empty

Campaigns with nothing yet. Primary control: New campaign. No sample tour, no fake rows.

work · campaigns

Work (table)

Campaign list as a table — name, audience count, status, last activity. Click a row to open setup or results.

setup · campaigns

Setup (list + draft rail)

One rail: audience / list left, message right. Merge fields like {{firstName}}. Connect mailbox or send one test to myself before full send.

done · inbox

Done (sent + reply)

Campaign marked sent. Inbox shows the reply on the contact thread. Optional booking link or call from that contact.
