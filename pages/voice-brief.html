Marketing · AI brief

Voice brief.

What you are reading: the delivery brief for Forwardslash marketing. It tells an AI (or a human) who we are talking to, what we offer at each step, how the conversation goes, and what the voice sounds like. Use it to write ads, letters, landings, and journey pages. It is not a customer page.

0 · Voice

How we sound

Swiss workshop: precise, calm, short sentences, concrete verbs. Peer to a founder who already works — not a SaaS brochure.

Use: mailbox, phone, list, booked meeting, own channel, one job, ten minutes, Swiss care.

Avoid: revolutionary, unlock next-level, crush it, cheaper than Close, seat-tax scare, $ in ads, feature dumps, countdown urgency.

Hang (every channel): Start free. Do one job.

Buttons only: Start free · Talk to us. Stickers live on Pricing — never invent dollars in letters or ads.

Full principles: design/!marketing/voice.md · scent: Brand scent.

1 · Who

Who we are talking to

Primary: founder or operator who already outbound from their own mailbox and phone. Firm size ~1–5. Geo AU / UK / IE. They care about finishing a job today, not touring a CRM.

Two cuts:

  • Solo — one human (e.g. Sydney founder). Proof ads only. Free first.
  • Essentials — ~five seats already sharing outbound. Paid CAC ok. Talk to us.

Not talking to: enterprise IT buyers, job seekers, “cheaper CRM” shoppers, people who want a demo farm.

2 · Steps

Who · offer · conversation · voice (each step)

Step A — Ad (Proof)

Who: Solo founder / small operator searching or on LinkedIn. Hasn’t loaded a list yet.

Offer: Do one free job from your mailbox and phone. No signup wall. CTA Start free.

Conversation: Ad nouns match the landing (mailbox, list, booking). One promise. They click → land on Upload a list or Free doors → optional email A1 if they bounce without a job.

Voice: One line hang or “Outbound from your mailbox.” No $. No cheaper-than-Close. Campaigns G1 / G2 / L1–L2 only — light budget, do not scale.

Step B — Free door (the one job)

Who: Same person, ready to try something small.

Offer: One of six free doors in ~10 minutes. The real “one job” is usually load a list and send/dial (Door 1) or publish a booking link (Door 2). Also: event signup, booking that emails, phone bot, investors/clients list.

Conversation: Show the door. Let them finish. Conversion = list loaded + first send/dial, or a shareable booking link — not a signup.

Voice: “You don’t need a workspace tour.” Name the job. Keep the artifact. Hang unchanged.

Step C — Follow-up letters (activate)

Who: Clicked but no job (seq A), or finished a door (seq B/C).

Offer: Next honest step only — ten sends/dials, or share the booking page with who replied. Cap 3 letters. Then stop.

Conversation: A1 → A2 day 3 → stop. If list live: B1 same day → B2 booking. If booking door: C1 → C2. One button. “Or talk to us” as plain text, never a second coloured CTA.

Voice: 3–6 lines. Subject = the job. No $. No “just checking in” fourth letter. Same calm as the site.

Step D — Solo polish (pay)

Who: Solo who already did the free job and wants cleaner send / branding off.

Offer: Solo annual (~$108 Close-parity) as polish. Monthly is flexibility tax — never the ad, never the letter. Story update in flight (FS-1582): Free keeps branding; Solo turns branding off — rewrite any “ads-off / unlock email” lines to match.

Conversation: Letter G1 only after they hit the need. “Keep the work. Pay for polish.” Button unlock polish — not Talk to us unless they ask.

Voice: Respect the work they already did. No founding bump. No second seat on Solo (that’s Essentials).

Step E — Seat / Essentials (Talk to us)

Who: ~5-person firm already outbound together. Company size ~11–50 on LinkedIn L3 / high-intent search.

Offer: Shared book, seats, sequences. Quote object $2,100 prepaid (5 × $35 × 12). CTA Talk to us. Only campaign we scale on CAC (G4).

Conversation: Land on Pricing → form or booked call → Sequence E (human reply same Sydney working day). Letters not hung yet — draft when told go.

Voice: Commercial honesty, not a demo farm. “I won’t undercut Close-shaped pricing on a call.” No Solo $108 on this cart. Founding deals not in the ad.

3 · How to use this

When you write or ship

  1. Lock who (Solo vs Essentials) and which step (A–E).
  2. Copy the offer and conversation for that step — don’t mix Proof and Seat.
  3. Write in the voice rules (section 0). Same nouns as the landing.
  4. Put $ only on Pricing / journey money strips — never in the ad.
