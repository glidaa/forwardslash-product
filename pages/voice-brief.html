2 · Overview

How marketing works (simple)

Someone hits an ad or a door. They do one small job in about ten minutes — load a list, share a booking page, and so on. That job is Free. If they want polish later (branding off on Solo, or seats on Essentials), they pay. Paid ads for Free alone don’t scale; paid CAC is for annual teams (Talk to us).

Two journeys matter right now:

  • Solo · Sydney — one founder. Ad G1. Free door first. Solo annual is polish.
  • Essentials · five seats — small firm. Ad G4 Talk to us. Quote object $2,100.

Full funnel map: Advertising & Journeys. Letters: Emails. Doors: Free doors. Ads copy: Ads pack · Ads plan.
