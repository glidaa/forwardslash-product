Marketing · build list

What the landing needs.

What you are reading: a checklist for Forwardslash landings, from Aircall + CloudTalk. Build brief — not a customer page.

1 · Above the fold

Must have

  1. Category line — what you are.
  2. Hero promise — outcome.
  3. Support line — how (one sentence).
  4. Primary CTA — Start free / trial.
  5. Secondary CTA — Talk to us / demo.
  6. Trust strip — logos or counts.
  7. Concrete free offer — name the free thing (CloudTalk: get an AI call + 50 AI minutes · 14-day trial · no card). Not a vague “start free.”
2 · Body

Blocks

  1. Pain / “can you relate” jobs.
  2. Pillars with nested features (parent → three explained children each).
  3. Offer ladder — Free → Solo → Essentials.
  4. Benefit → feature.
  5. Proof — quotes / metrics.
  6. Integrations.
  7. Mid-page CTA pair.
  8. FAQ.
  9. Bottom CTA pair.
3 · Site chrome

Nav sections

  1. Product overview.
  2. Solutions — top uses · by industry · by team/size.
  3. Pricing.
  4. Resources & docs — Help Center, API/docs, blog/hubs, guides.
  5. Partners & affiliates — meet partners, channel partner, affiliate partner, tech partners.
  6. Legal center (Aircall pattern) — T&Cs, Website ToU, EULA; Privacy Policy, DPA, Privacy FAQ, Subprocessors; telecom/CPNI if voice; API license for tech partners; taxes/fees by region if needed.
  7. Security badges when true.
4 · Competitor notes

Hang patterns

Aircall: communications evolved · Team-led / AI-assisted / AI-led nested · Try for free / Talk to sales · Legal + Partners + Resources.

CloudTalk: “AI business phone for sales and support” · “Every call answered 24/7 — team or AI” · Book a Demo / Start Free Trial · 50 AI minutes free + get a call from the AI.

Us: outbound home (mailbox + phone + list → meeting). Steal structure; keep own-channel voice. Hang must name the free foothold.
