Ads operating plan v0.1

How the ads run.

Copy stays in the ads pack. This is the machine: four campaigns, matching landings, honest conversions. Paid CAC only for annual teams. Do not buy traffic just for Free.

Before spend

Ship gates

Destination

This book, or a landing in this repo. Visible H1. Not the forwardslash.ch empty-state. Not gamma until the white-on-white H1 is dead.

Scent

Ad nouns already on the page: mailbox, phone, booked meeting. Hang: Start free. Do one job.

Hygiene

Negatives loaded. No cheap CRM. No cheaper than Close. No jobs. No enterprise IT theatre.

One promise

One campaign, one landing, UTM on every URL. No stacked offers. No $ in the ad.

Two buckets

Proof is light. Seat is the CAC.

Proof

Show a door. CTA Start free. Conversion = list loaded, or a booking page with a shareable link. Do not scale this to farm Free.

Seat

Annual Essentials, Growth, or Enterprise. CTA Talk to us. Conversion = form or a booked call. This is the only campaign that may grow on CAC.

Four campaigns

What we actually run

G1 Proof

Own mailbox

Google RSA. Pin “Outbound from your mailbox” or the hang. Land on Upload a list. Email seq A, then B if they load a list.

G2 Proof

Booking page

Google RSA. Pin “Booking page in ten minutes.” Land on One action or Free doors. Email seq C.

G3 Mix

LinkedIn image

Machined slash, paper field. L1/L2 at 1–10 Start free. L3 at 11–50 Talk to us, land on Pricing.

G4 Seat

Talk to us

High-intent search + L3. Land on Pricing. Email seq E, human reply same working day. The only one we scale.

15s cutdown later, same nouns as G1. Retarget only people who hit the book and did not load a list. Event / phone-bot sitelinks only when those URLs exist.
