Hang

Start free. Do one job.

One promise. Free is a real job in about ten minutes — load a list, publish a booking page, put the first send or dial on the contact. Paid is polish and seats when the firm is ready. Never a tour. Never cheaper-than-Close.

Visual

Black and paper. No chroma. Radius 9. Space Grotesk for display, Inter for body. Grey is hierarchy only. One invert band per scroll beat max.

Tone

Swiss workshop. Short sentences. Concrete verbs: send, dial, book, measure. Peer to a founder who already works — not a SaaS brochure.
