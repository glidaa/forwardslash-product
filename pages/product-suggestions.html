What the field is selling

Phone system / dialler

Numbers, click-to-call, power / parallel dial, recording, coaching, IVR and queues. Peers: Aircall, CloudTalk, JustCall, Ringover, Dialpad.

Outbound email machine

Sequences, warmup, unlimited mailboxes, master inbox, agents that write and reply. Peers: Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist, Woodpecker, Mailshake.

Marketing automation hub

Journeys, lists, ads, attribution, ecommerce triggers. Peers: Brevo, Mailchimp, HubSpot Marketing, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo.

Sales CRM suite

Pipeline, forecasting, CPQ, seats, AI that updates records. Peers: Close, Pipedrive, HubSpot Sales, Salesforce, Zoho, Dynamics.

Work OS / delivery

Tasks, boards, docs, client portals. Peers: ClickUp, Asana, monday, Jira, Notion, Basecamp, Teamwork.

AI agents layer

Voice agents, reply agents, playbooks, CSAT bots — bolted onto phone or CRM. Appears across diallers and CRMs.

Priorities

Add or change

P1 — do soon

  1. Make deliverability a first-class paid story. Catalog already has Deliverability care. Competitors win the sentence (“warmup / placement”). Spell the Free→Solo polish path without selling private IP farms or DFY mailboxes.
  2. Number reputation polish on dialler. Branded caller ID / number health show up as gaps vs CloudTalk/JustCall. Small surface; big trust for AU/UK/IE outbound. Not IVR.
  3. Name the AI jobs in product UI. We have the capabilities (draft, summarize, call notes, phone bot, MCP). Peers sell named agents. Keep “assist the work” — give plain job names, not a robot mascot.
  4. Shared / master inbox clarity. Essentials+ shared inbox exists. Outbound peers hang “one place for replies across domains.” Make that the team story next to own-mailbox send.

P2 — strengthen what we claim

  1. Parallel / multi-line dial as Scale language if usage economics allow — peers sell it as add-on; we already have predictive at Scale.
  2. Campaign experiment language — A/B (or A/Z) shows as gap vs Instantly/ActiveCampaign. Keep it simple: variants on one campaign, not a marketing suite.
  3. Agency shared workspace — Smartlead/Woodpecker white-label is a gap we map to shared workspace. Deepen client approval + workspace, refuse full white-label farms.
  4. Free proof that can be felt — doors already exist; keep them as concrete as CloudTalk’s “50 AI minutes / get a call” without copying support demos.

P3 — change / sharpen (not new modules)

  1. Refuse list stays visible — IVR/ACD, send farms, LinkedIn automation as core, CPQ/forecasting suites, ads managers. Do not let partial tags drag us into building them.
  2. Close custom objects / group numbers / funnel reports — treat as “enough with custom fields, round robin, stages, overview,” not a roadmap to Close clones.
  3. Work tools — no new boards; keep tasks on contacts and delivery projects.
