Product · look system

Look.

From the Prism design system. Ink, paper, black. Space Grotesk + Inter. Craft and quiet confidence — not gradients, not AI purple, not postcard Alps.

Do

Grey as hierarchy only. One invert block (black fill / white type) per scroll beat. Hover: border → #111, never glow.

Don’t

Gradients-as-brand, soft pastels, blob illustrations, glassmorphism, fake “AI purple”, warm cream kits.

Type & structure

Type and rhythm

Type

Display: Space Grotesk 500/600 (h1–h3, brand, prices). Tracking ≈ −0.035em.
Body / UI: Inter 400/500/600. Body 14px / 1.55.
Eyebrow: 9–10px, tracking ~0.14–0.17em, uppercase. One idea per headline.

Structure

Max ~1280 · wrap ~1100 · rail 232 · sticky top 64 · section pad ~65. Cards radius 9 · buttons radius 6 · hairline 1px. Prefer 2/3 splits; 8px align; generous air.

Product UI feel

Features rail and journeys

Large figures. Quiet captions. The Features browser should feel like a catalog you can skim — group ledes short, references on the side, not a brochure inside the rail. Journey pages: one person, one next step, same hang.

Optional research habit when framing a surface (not law, not jargon): empty → work → detail → done → two comps. That is enough to see a page. We do not invent a “six frames” recipe as product doctrine.
