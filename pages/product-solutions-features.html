1 · Events

Events

Start on page: Event outreach H1. Primary Start free → create Event / Booking page (branded Free). Secondary Talk to us.

First screen after start: Event page (or Booking link) empty — title, date, signup. Side path: upload attendee CSV into a list on the event.

Features that help: Event page · Booking links · Booking page · Public page · Forms on pages · Page blocks · Lists · CSV upload · Contact book · Own-mailbox send · Templates · Reminders · Timezone care · Campaigns (attendee follow-up) · Calendar sync (later).

Packages that fit:

  • Free — natural start. Branded Event / Booking / multi-page + light own-mailbox follow-up.
  • Solo — polish. Branding off; Page blocks · Forms · Custom domain · Ads off.
  • Essentials — team event. Shared inbox for RSVP replies · Round robin · Owner & team.
  • Growth — volume follow-up. Sequences + bulk after the event list is warm.
  • Scale — not the door default. Only if high-volume dial / predictive sits on the same book.

Channels: landing · ads · email · webinar invite

Out of path / don’t stuff into First campaign: Round robin · Power dialler · Sequences volume · Predictive.

2 · Marketing

Marketing

Start on page: Outbound book for campaigns + pages. Start free → New campaign (or Pages). Talk to us for team.

First screen after start: Campaigns empty + connect mailbox. Alternate: Public page empty (branded Free multi-page).

Features that help: Campaigns · Own-mailbox send · Gmail sync · Outlook / Microsoft sync · Lists · CSV upload · Templates · Public page · Page blocks · Contact book · Activity timeline · Open & click tracking · Shared inbox · Campaign approval · Snippets · Scheduled send.

Packages that fit:

  • Free — foothold. Branded multi-page + branded own-mailbox campaign links.
  • Solo — natural solo marketer. Branding off on email & pages · Templates · bigger CSV.
  • Essentials — natural team. Shared inbox · Bulk tailored · Campaign approval · Open & click.
  • Growth — active outreach. Sequences + bulk · Power dial if voice joins the same campaign.
  • Scale — control later. Roles / visibility when the book gets large.

Channels: landing · ads · email

Out of path / don’t stuff into First campaign: Bulk Growth · Sequences as hero · Campaign approval (own Essentials journey) · Power dialler.

3 · Emailing

Emailing

Start on page: Send B2B email from your mailbox. Start free → Connect Google or SMTP. Same as First campaign Free rail.

First screen after start: Connect mailbox → New campaign → list or write-first → send → Inbox.

Features that help: Gmail sync · Outlook / Microsoft sync · Own-mailbox send · Campaigns · CSV upload · Lists · Contact book · Templates (light Free) · Snippets · Scheduled send · Unsubscribe & compliance · Inbox · Internal notes · Import contacts · Activity timeline.

Packages that fit:

  • Free — natural fit / First campaign rail. Branded own-mailbox send + capped list/CSV + campaign links.
  • Solo — branding off. Clean mailbox send · Templates · Deliverability care · bigger files.
  • Essentials — team replies. Shared inbox · @ mentions · Open & click · Campaign approval.
  • Growth — volume. Sequences + bulk email.
  • Scale — not the door default.

Channels: landing · ads · email

Out of path / don’t stuff into First campaign: Sequences · Bulk · Shared inbox · Dialler (sitelink tease only) · Branding-off as a Free promise.

4 · Finding leads

Finding leads

Start on page: Build or load a list you can work. Start free → Lists / CSV (or Name search). Not enrichment-first in the hero.

First screen after start: Lists empty — Upload CSV or New list. Soft: Name search. Then hand into Campaigns.

Features that help: Lists · CSV upload · Contact book · Company records · Import contacts · Name search · Tags & segments · List dedupe · Enrich a list · Find email · Find phone · Market lists · Smart views · Saved searches · Company lookup · Campaigns (next after list) · Suppression.

Packages that fit:

  • Free — natural start. One capped list · soft Name search · hand into branded send/pages.
  • Solo — natural operator. Bigger CSV · List dedupe · Market lists · Smart views · Export.
  • Essentials — shared book. Assign leads · Owner & team · Shared workspace.
  • Growth — list ops. Bulk edit · Lead scoring · Sequences once the list is ready.
  • Scale — visibility rules when many seats share the same research book.

Channels: landing · ads · email · webinar invite (optional — list-build → event invite)

Out of path / don’t stuff into First campaign: Enrichment-first hero · Lead scoring · Assign leads · Bulk edit as hero · Paid Find email/phone as the Start free empty state.

5 · B2B marketing

B2B marketing

Start on page: Mailbox + list + pages for B2B. Primary Talk to us (Essentials desk / OTO page). Start free = Free taste foothold only — not the money story.

First screen after start: Same Free rail as Emailing/Marketing; pages sitelink for branded multi-page. Proof: Inbox / Activity timeline.

Features that help: Own-mailbox send · Campaigns · Lists · CSV upload · Templates · Public page · Contact book · Activity timeline · Research briefs · Sequences · Booking page after replies · Open & click · Unsubscribe & compliance · Company records.

Packages that fit:

  • Free — foothold. Branded own-mailbox + multi-page proof.
  • Solo — natural solo B2B. Branding off · Templates · Market lists · Research briefs (credits).
  • Essentials — team B2B. Shared inbox · Campaign approval · Open & click.
  • Growth — natural active door. Sequences + bulk · Sequence suggestions.
  • Scale — later control when roles/visibility matter.

Channels: landing · ads · email · webinar invite (optional)

Out of path / don’t stuff into First campaign: Essentials approval as hero · Growth sequences as the Start free empty state · Power dialler.

6 · B2B calling

B2B calling

Start on page: Call from the same book as email. Primary Talk to us (OTO page). Start free = email foothold only; dialler sitelink tease — not the Free empty state.

First screen after start (call path): List or Contact with Click to call. People first — CSV/Lists before dial queue.

Features that help: Lists · CSV upload · Contact book · Click to call · Call notes · Dispositions · Local numbers · Call recording · Power dialler · Own-mailbox send · Campaigns (same book) · Activity timeline · Voicemail drop · Call note assist.

Packages that fit:

  • Free — foothold only. List + contact book; dial is not the Free hero. Mailbox/list first.
  • Solo — light call. Click to call · Call notes · Dispositions · Local numbers (usage).
  • Essentials — shared follow-up. Shared inbox for mail after the call · Assign leads.
  • Growth — natural dial door. Power dialler · Voicemail drop · Sequences that mix send + dial.
  • Scale — high volume. Predictive dialler · Live coaching.

Channels: landing · ads · email

Out of path / don’t stuff into First campaign: Predictive · Live coaching · Voicemail drop · Phone bot as lead · Power dial as Free Start free empty state.

7 · Team of callers

Team of callers

Start on page: One book for a calling team. Primary Talk to us (Essentials/Growth). Start free = foothold only — do not stuff seats into First campaign.

First screen after start (paid path): Shared workspace / Assign leads · Power dialler queue · Shared inbox for follow-up mail. Manager: dispositions / Campaign approval.

Features that help: Power dialler · Assign leads · Owner & team · Shared inbox · Shared workspace · @ mentions · Team presence · Dispositions · Call notes · Lists · Click to call · Campaign approval · Live coaching · Predictive dialler · Visibility rules · Task assign.

Packages that fit:

  • Free — not this door. Foothold mailbox/list only; never position as Free First campaign.
  • Solo — not the team fit. One operator; wrong shape for a calling floor.
  • Essentials — natural team floor. Shared workspace · Shared inbox · Assign leads · Owner & team · Campaign approval.
  • Growth — natural active floor. Power dialler + Sequences · Voicemail drop · Bulk.
  • Scale — floor at volume. Predictive · Live coaching · Visibility rules · Audit trail.

Channels: landing · ads · email · Talk to us heavy (usually no webinar invite)

Out of path / don’t stuff into First campaign: This door is Essentials/Growth (Scale later) journey — not Path A/B/C. No Free First rail seats. No Start free empty state that assumes Power dial without Talk to us.
