Competition · Landing Competition · Pricing
Peer

Close

Close is the shape of our pricing and the nearest sales CRM peer: Solo / Essentials / Growth / Scale, calling + email on the contact, no forever-free. We match the ladder stickers. We win with a real Free job Close will not give, own-channel honesty, and one book through delivery — not by undercutting.

Like: plan grammar buyers already understand; named AI that acts on the book; self-serve trial.

Refuse: becoming Salesforce-replacement theatre; centering an AI clone over mailbox and phone.

By pack

What we like · what we refuse · gaps vs catalog

Diallers

Aircall, CloudTalk, JustCall, Ringover, Dialpad. Strong on numbers, recording, coaching, AI voice agents.

Like: concrete free proof (minutes, get a call); dual Start free / Talk to sales; nested job → feature pillars; number + local presence.

Refuse: contact-center as the category (IVR / ACD / wallboards / CSAT / fax as identity). We are outbound, not a support phone system.

Vs FS: we already catalog click-to-call, power / predictive, recording, notes, coaching, phone bot. Real gaps they show are mostly routing/IVR we do not want, plus number reputation / branded caller ID worth considering as polish — not a new category.

Outbound email

Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist, Woodpecker, Mailshake. Volume, warmup infra, agents, multichannel.

Like: “AI outbound” category clarity; named agents; master inbox for replies; dialler next to email; free start / trial + demo doors.

Refuse: send farms, unlimited mailbox theatre, DFY mailbox shops, LinkedIn automation as core identity, private IP rotation as the whole story.

Vs FS: own-mailbox send, sequences, campaigns, deliverability care, shared inbox are in the catalog. Gaps they advertise (SISR, SmartServers, buy-mailboxes) are infra we should not become. Strengthen deliverability as a first-class paid story without farming inboxes.

Marketing hubs

Brevo, Mailchimp, HubSpot Marketing, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo.

Like: journey clarity; free footholds; attribution language when it stays honest.

Refuse: ads managers, social inboxes, ecommerce loyalty wallets, SEO/blog suites as our product. That is not the outbound book.

Vs FS: campaigns + tracking cover the useful slice. Push, retargeting ads, multi-touch marketing attribution are out of scope for now.

CRMs

Salesforce, HubSpot Sales, Pipedrive, Close, Zoho, Dynamics.

Like: HubSpot’s forever-free foothold (ours = one Free job); Pipedrive simplicity hang; Close ladder.

Refuse: CPQ, forecasting suites, partner management, LinkedIn Sales Navigator as required glue, custom-object sprawl as the pitch.

Vs FS: contact book, stages, ownership, reporting-lite are enough. Do not grow a second Salesforce. Custom fields stay; custom-object platforms do not.

Work tools

ClickUp, Asana, monday, Jira, Notion, Basecamp, Teamwork.

Like: quiet task surfaces, client-facing delivery spaces, short checklists on the job.

Refuse: becoming a general work OS. Tasks sit on contacts and delivery projects — not a second monday.com.

Vs FS: todo + delivery + overview already map. Almost no catalog gaps; keep them thin.

Things we like

One synthesis

  1. Close ladder — Solo / Essentials / Growth / Scale as shared commercial grammar.
  2. Free that feels real — minutes, a call, a list, a booking link — not “start free” with nothing behind it.
  3. Nested jobs — parent jobs, child features (Aircall shape), not a flat dump.
  4. Own channel + agents — AI that assists on the book; mailbox and phone stay yours.
  5. Outcome chain — list → send/dial → reply → booked meeting, said plainly.
