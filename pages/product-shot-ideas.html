Product · design next

Shot ideas.

Each shot is a screen that makes a feature decision obvious. Competition = evidence. Catalog = what you already claim. These shots are what to design next so you can see the gap.

Method

How to use

Shot

One UI frame — empty, work, detail, or done. Enough to judge the feature without a full flow.

Feature question

What product choice the shot forces. If you cannot answer Have / Partial / Gap after the frame, the shot is wrong.

Vs catalog

Have = already in Features. Partial = claimed but thin in story or UI. Gap = missing or not named. Design the Partial and Gap frames first.

Surface

Inbox, campaigns, dialler, CRM, pages — same order as Surfaces for jpg packs.

P1

Design these first

From Suggestions. Four themes. Each gets concrete frames you can sketch or brief.

1. Deliverability story

Own-mailbox send health — not farm warmup. Catalog has Deliverability care; peers win the sentence.

Partial · inbox

Mailbox health empty

See: Connected mailbox card with DNS / SPF / DKIM / DMARC as plain checks — grey until verified. No “buy more inboxes.”

Proves: Deliverability care is a real surface, not a footnote. You already claim it; this shot makes Free→Solo polish visible.

Surface: inbox

Partial · inbox

Send health work

See: One mailbox mid-week — placement score, bounce rate, “pause sends?” cue. Own domain only.

Proves: Reputation is part of the product during work, not a setup wizard you forget.

Surface: inbox

Partial · campaigns

Campaign blocked on health

See: Campaign ready to launch; send blocked or warned because mailbox health failed a check. Approval still required.

Proves: Deliverability sits next to campaigns — not a separate “warmup product.”

Surface: campaigns

Have · inbox

Own-mailbox done

See: First campaign sent from the user’s Google/Microsoft mailbox. Thread lands on the contact. No private IP theatre.

Proves: Own-mailbox send already exists — keep it the hero; health is the polish layer.

Surface: inbox

2. Number reputation

Calling identity — branded CID / number health. Gap vs CloudTalk/JustCall polish. Not IVR.

Gap · dialler

Number health empty

See: Assigned AU/UK/IE number with empty reputation strip — “not checked yet,” local presence toggle off.

Proves: You need a number-identity surface. Catalog has dialler; reputation is the missing sentence.

Surface: dialler

Gap · dialler

Outbound with branded CID

See: Dial workspace showing what the callee sees (business name / local number). One line of trust copy, no wallboard.

Proves: Branded caller ID / local presence as product choice — small surface, big trust for outbound.

Surface: dialler

Partial · dialler

Call done on contact

See: Call ended; recording + notes on the contact timeline. Number used shown once.

Proves: Notes-on-contact already Have; reputation is additive polish, not a new phone system.

Surface: dialler / crm

3. Named AI jobs

Assist specific tasks — draft, summarize, call notes, phone bot. Not a purple AI blob. Capabilities Have; naming is Partial.

Partial · inbox

Draft reply job

See: Thread open; control labelled “Draft reply” (not “Ask AI”). Preview in your voice; accept / edit / discard.

Proves: Named job = catalog Draft replies made obvious in UI.

Surface: inbox / ai

Partial · inbox

Thread summary job

See: Long thread collapsed to three bullets labelled “Summarise thread.” No mascot.

Proves: Thread summaries exist — name them like a tool, not a robot.

Surface: inbox / ai

Partial · dialler

Call notes job

See: After hang-up, “Write call notes” fills the contact. Human edits before save.

Proves: Assist-the-work after the dial — not a voice-agent marketing panel.

Surface: dialler / crm

Have · dialler

Phone bot detail

See: Phone bot as a named job with bounds (what it can say / when it hands off). Still assist, not “run the firm.”

Proves: Phone bot is catalogued — keep the frame quiet and bounded.

Surface: dialler / ai

4. Shared / master inbox clarity

Essentials+ shared inbox exists. Peers sell “one place for replies across domains.” Make the team story next to own-mailbox send.

Partial · inbox

Shared queue empty

See: Team inbox with zero open replies. Filters: mine / shared / unassigned. Domains listed as sources, not farms.

Proves: Shared inbox is a first-class queue — not buried under personal mail.

Surface: inbox

Partial · inbox

Master reply work

See: Replies from two domains in one list; assign to seat; open thread on the contact.

Proves: “One place for replies” without Instantly-style unlimited mailbox theatre.

Surface: inbox

Have · inbox

Reply done on contact

See: Answered from shared queue; activity on the person; owner updated if needed.

Proves: Shared mail still lands on the book — CRM and inbox stay one story.

Surface: inbox / crm

5. Parallel dial language

Scale language if economics allow. Predictive already at Scale; peers sell parallel as named add-on.

Partial · dialler

Parallel dial work

See: Dialler with multi-line status — lines ringing, one connected, others drop cleanly. Label: parallel / multi-line, not “AI dialler.”

Proves: Whether Scale needs a named parallel mode beside predictive.

Surface: dialler

Have · dialler

Power dial empty

See: Queue loaded, one-at-a-time power dial ready. Contrast frame so parallel is a clear step-up, not a rename.

Proves: Power dial already Have — parallel is language + control, not a new category.

Surface: dialler

6. Campaign A/B

Variants on one campaign — not a marketing suite. Gap vs Instantly/ActiveCampaign experiment language.

Gap · campaigns

Variant setup empty

See: Campaign editor with A / B subject or body slots. Same list, same approval path.

Proves: Simple experiment language — do we add A/B or stay single-send?

Surface: campaigns

Gap · campaigns

Variant result done

See: One campaign result view — A vs B opens / replies. Winner marked. No attribution waterfall.

Proves: Enough reporting to decide the next send without HubSpot Marketing sprawl.

Surface: campaigns

7. Agency workspace / approval

Deepen client approval + shared workspace. Refuse full white-label farms. Catalog has Campaign approval + Client approval.

Partial · campaigns

Client approval desk

See: Client-facing approval of copy before send. Comment + approve. Agency seat vs client seat clear.

Proves: Approval desk is real product — strengthen, don’t invent white-label portals.

Surface: campaign-approval / campaigns

Partial · crm

Shared workspace detail

See: Agency workspace with client book scoped — who sees which contacts. No “unlimited client portals” pitch.

Proves: Shared workspace as the agency answer to Smartlead white-label.

Surface: crm / admin

8. Free proof you can feel

Doors already exist. Keep them as concrete as CloudTalk’s “50 AI minutes / get a call.”

Have · pages

Booking page done

See: Published booking page after ~ten minutes. Link copied. First Free job felt.

Proves: Free door energy — pages surface carries the proof.

Surface: pages / meetings

Have · crm

List loaded work

See: Import ≤500 contacts; first send or dial from a row. No tour overlay.

Proves: “Start free. Do one job.” is a screen, not a slogan.

Surface: crm / list-building

Partial · dialler

Get a call proof

See: Empty Free dial strip with one concrete affordance — place a test call / claim minutes. Quiet, not a support demo clone.

Proves: Whether Free calling needs a felt proof beat like dialler peers.

Surface: dialler

P3

Skip as product shots

Refuse list — do not brief frames that drag us into modules we are not building.

  • IVR / ACD — contact-center identity; we are outbound, not queues and wallboards.
  • Send farms — private IP / unlimited mailbox infra; refuse the category.
  • LinkedIn automation core — not our channel identity; keep LinkedIn out of the shot pack.
  • CPQ / forecasting — enterprise CRM theatre; enough with stages and overview.
  • Ads managers — marketing-suite sprawl; campaigns + pages cover the useful slice.
