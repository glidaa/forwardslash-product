Questions

About joining sales and delivery.

Does a campaign replace an opportunity pipeline?

No. Campaign engagement is an attribution funnel. Opportunities retain their own configurable commercial lifecycle.

Does every won opportunity need a project?

No. Create a board when there is work to coordinate. Simple transactions can close without manufacturing project overhead.

Can customers see internal CRM information?

Customer sharing is bounded to the approved status, tasks, files and decisions. Internal notes and unrelated records remain private.

Can delivery users work without a specialist licence?

Yes. Business collaborators can use CRM, projects, tasks, approval, inbox and reporting. Specialist licences are for bulk campaigns and power calling.
