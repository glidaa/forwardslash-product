Pricing questions

Definitions before purchase.

Why is Business A$249?

It funds a shared CRM, inbox, campaign planning, approvals, projects, reporting, API and governance for unlimited collaborators. The earlier A$79 proposal undervalued that complete company system.

What does “unlimited collaborators” exclude?

It excludes specialist bulk campaign and power-calling acceleration, exceptional supplier usage, abuse and capacity beyond published reasonable-use limits.

Can we remove licences mid-month?

The intended billing design supports licence reassignment and cancellation while preserving activity ownership and history.

Are these final public prices?

No. They are explicit launch hypotheses to validate with design partners before making a binding public offer.
