Why A$249 rather than A$79?

A$79 undervalued a shared CRM, delivery system, inbox, approvals, reporting and API with unlimited collaborators. A$249 is still accessible to a small company, while supporting a serious product and removing the incentive to charge every person a seat.

Questions

Start without uncertainty.

Do I have to choose the “right” starting point?

No. Email, lists, projects and campaigns all connect to the same underlying company and contact context.

Does connecting my mailbox expose all my email?

The product direction calls for explicit scope and clear personal-versus-shared boundaries. A company billing transfer must never silently expose private mail.

What is a campaign licence?

It is software for the named people who build and run bulk audiences and sequences. Ordinary one-to-one email and collaboration remain part of the workspace.

Can we run forwardslash.ch beside our existing CRM?

Yes. Lists, bounded projects, export and API paths are intended to let a small team prove value before any replacement decision.
