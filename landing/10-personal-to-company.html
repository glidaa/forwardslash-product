Questions

Starting personally.

Can I pay personally and expense it?

Yes. Business should provide a normal receipt and later transfer billing to a company administrator without recreating the workspace.

What happens when a trial ends?

Paid execution pauses safely. Your free records, tasks, drafts, history and export remain.

Will free emails contain advertising?

A small forwardslash.ch attribution may appear on free campaign or public-page surfaces, shown in preview. It should not be inserted into ordinary personal replies inside existing threads.

Can the company claim my workspace?

Not silently. Domain verification can begin a consolidation proposal, but ownership and private-data boundaries require explicit review.
