## Ban list (quick)

**Hype:** revolutionary · game-changing · magic · unlock · 10x · crush · hack · hypergrowth · rocket · moonshot · overnight pipeline  
**Urgency theatre:** countdown · limited-time spam · stacked CTAs · fake scarcity  
**Undercut:** cheaper than Close · A$249 workspace · cheap CRM · undercut stickers · managed ~$1,950 outbound as a hook  
**Alps postcard:** summit your revenue · alpine-grade · tourist peaks · emoji mountains · hustle grind  
**Pricing:** hardcoded $ · “only $19/mo” as the ad · seat-tax scare math  
**Free shorthand:** “ads on, uploads off” as the whole offer  
**Proof lies:** vanity logos, inflated AI, invented metrics

Hero / email / video / free-doors drafts stay frozen.
