## Brief

**Thesis.** Outbound from your mailbox and phone. One system from first touch to booked meeting. Swiss care, not CRM theatre.

**Michael’s ask (kept).** Simple connection to people. A different message in each script. Rethink from the todo-app era to Forwardslash now — list still starts the journey; the job is reach, not chores.

**Free.** No signup. Share via link. One job in about ten minutes. Pay later for email-send polish and serious uploads. Stickers live in `design/!pricing`.

**Visual (all 13).** Machined slash `/` / piton-scale hardware / paper field / quiet ink-paper UI. **Not** Envato stock. **Not** literal Alps scenery. **Not** meditation stock. **Not** jungle stock. **Not** stock people.

**Founder mechanic.** Slash-A assign (`/` then A + name) is original product language. Not re-verified in current code. Keep as: *type `/` then the person* — labelled founder mechanic where it appears (scripts 1 and 5).
