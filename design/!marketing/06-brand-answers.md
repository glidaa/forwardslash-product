## 1. Journey / list philosophy

### Honour the opening

All journeys start with a step. An idea written in a list. A plan. Simple list → assign tasks, build a team, track, run projects. It starts by writing the list. Start now.

### On voice (locked rewrite)

Every journey starts with a step. An idea, written as a list. A plan you can hold.

Forwardslash starts there. Write the list. No signup. About ten minutes. One real job.

From that list you assign tasks. You invite a colleague. You track what moved. You run the project when the work is ready — not when a CRM is fully configured.

It starts by writing the list. Start free. Do one job.

### Overwhelm

Intake: break the deliverable into tangible steps; follow the playbook; small steps; do not add unnecessary stress.

On voice: Overwhelm is a pile without a sequence. Name the next step. Then the one after. The playbook is the list. Practice is how the work gets quieter. Do not add a second system while you are still lost in the first. Small steps. Start now.

### Elephant

How do you eat an elephant? One bite at a time.

On voice: One job. One list. One seam. Load names. Send or dial. Book. Measure. The whole animal is not the first sitting. Start free. Do one job.

### Happier at work

Intake prompt: why are you happier at work when you would rather be at home.

Answer: You would rather be at home because work has become a house of tools. Logins. Seat counts. Send farms. Dashboards that do not match the mailbox you actually use. Home is quiet. Work should be quiet enough to finish a thought.

Happier at work is not a perk and not a slogan. It is owned work versus chaos. Your mailbox. Your numbers. One list. The next step named. You can leave the desk without a pile of logins in your head.

### How the journey connects to Free doors

The list *is* the free membership. No signup. One job in about ten minutes *is* the door.

| Journey step | Free door | First artifact |
|--------------|-----------|----------------|
| Write the list | Load a list → email + call from their phone | Live list + first send or dial from *their* channels |
| Make a plan others can use | Booking / calendar page | Shareable booking link |
| Put a date on the work | Event that emails on signup | Event + signup list |
| Close the loop | Booking that emails | Booked slot that triggered an email |
| Hear the call seam | Phone bot on one number | One number live, one logged attempt |
| Keep the relationships | Track investors / clients as list + tasks | Named list + next actions |

Link-share stays free. Pay when email-send polish or serious uploads matter. Stickers → `design/!pricing`.

## 3. Plain English, long form

*(For a reader with little English. Simple. Still Swiss. Not slang.)*

Forwardslash is a tool for small firms. You send email from your own mailbox. You call from your own phone. You keep a list of people. You book meetings. All of this lives in one quiet system.

Many tools send mail from a place you do not own. They call from numbers that are not yours. Then they show you a report and ask you to trust it. Forwardslash does not work that way. Your mailbox. Your numbers. Your list.

You start with a list. You write names. You write the next step. You do not need an account to try this. In about ten minutes you can finish one real job. You can load a short list and email or call from your phone. You can make a booking page and share the link. You can put up a small event and email the people who sign up. You can try a phone helper on one number. You can track investors or clients as a list with tasks.

The list is the start. From the list you give work to people. You invite a colleague. You see what is done. You can run a full project when you are ready. You do not buy a large system first.

When work is many tools, it is noisy. It follows you home. When work is one list you own, it is quieter. You know the next step. You can leave the desk without a pile of logins in your head.

We do not run your outbound for you. We do not send from a hidden farm. We do not tax you a new seat every time you hire. Paid plans follow a shape many teams already know. The numbers live in the pricing pack. We do not sell fear. We do not sell a cheaper copy of someone else. We sell one system from the first touch to a booked meeting, built with care.

Start free. Do one job. Or talk to us if you need seats and sequences now.

That is the whole offer. A step. A list. Your channels. A meeting on the calendar. Swiss care, not theatre.
