## Journey

All journeys start with a step. An idea that you write down in a list. A plan for how to get there. We make that easy with a simple list, where you can put your plan. When you are ready you can assign tasks off the list, build your team, track how you are going and run full projects. It starts by writing your list of things to do. Start now.

> If you are feeling overwhelmed, break down your deliverable into the most tangible steps, no matter how small. Follow the steps to complete the larger goals. Follow the playbook to win the game. In time, with practice you will learn to manage the steps and navigate better. Don't add unnecessary stress on yourself, just focus on the small steps.

How do you eat an elephant — one bite at a time.
