## 1. Reinvented the todo list

**Angle:** List first. Type forward slash, then A, then the name. Share without signup.

We have reinvented the todo list. Forward Slash. This is a simple application to organize your day-to-day work.

How does it work? It is simple: start by creating a list. Now you have a simple list of tasks, but if you need to assign one, just type forward slash then A and the name of the person you are sharing with. You can share a link to tasks and your team doesn't need to sign up. Or put an email to send a task, upload files and start a conversation about the work.

You can share files, comment on a task, write requirements — whatever you need. As your project grows, you have a simple way to see due dates, progress, and talk about tasks without any setup or IT approvals. You can send tasks to people outside your company and easily keep on a project.

With forwardSlash, you work with a simple, fast and efficient process. List the pending tasks in a line of work, so you can see who has pending work and who does not.

Work simply. Start today with Forwardslash by making a list of your outstanding tasks.

## 5. Twenty minutes, twice

**Angle:** Prioritize. Assign. Last 20 minutes of the day; first 20 of the morning.

Life can be full of distractions with urgent tasks, lots of to-dos, and missed appointments. But who can handle all that chaos? Well, you.

Prioritize the list to make sure you are facing your most valuable problems first, then seek help by assigning tasks. You only need to visit them each day. Always spend the last 20 minutes of the day planning the next one and the first 20 minutes reviewing what you intend to do. If you don’t have a plan, then write off the day and just plan the next day.

Forwardslash lets you create a list and write all you need to do. You will be able to prioritize these lists to make sure you are keeping the most important things top of mind. You can ask your coworkers to help you review the lists and only go to these tasks once a day.

It takes no more than twenty minutes at the end of the day to assign to-dos. Then, at the beginning of each day, take a few minutes to review what you said you would do. If you end up with no pending tasks, then great! A free day with no worries until tomorrow.

Start dealing with your day by going to Forwardslash.ch app right now.
