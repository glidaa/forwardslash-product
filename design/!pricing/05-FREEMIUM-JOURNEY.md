# Freemium journey — anonymous → pay

**Updated 24 Aug 2026 (fs-prism).**  
Michael’s gate: **no sign-in** for core craft (tasks, page, name search). **Pay** when they need email identity or serious file upload. List upload + link-share can stay free to create endowment.

Currency: Close stickers (USD). Bootstrap CAC still **$1–2k** for *paid* acquisition — this journey is mostly **organic / product-led**.

---

## 0. Gate map (what’s free vs paid)

| Action | Sign-in? | Cost | Why free / why paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create / run **tasks** | No | $0 | Hook — feel the system without friction |
| **Build a page** (booking / public page) | No | $0 | Endowment — something they’ve made |
| **Search for names** | No | $0 | Research wedge; proves “find” |
| **Upload a list** (CSV, modest cap) | No (or soft account later) | $0 | Bigger endowment; shareable artifact |
| **Share list/page via link** | No | $0 | Viral / collaboration without email |
| **Share via email** / send from product | Soft account → **add email** | Still free until send volume? **Pay to remove ads + unlock paid send** | Identity + deliverability = commercial surface |
| **Upload files** (attachments, larger / repeat imports) | Yes | **Paid** (Solo+) | Cost + abuse; classic upgrade wall |
| Remove **ads** on emails + public pages | — | **Paid** | Professional identity threat |
| Sequences / dialler / team | — | Essentials / Growth / Scale | Power features |

**Working rule:** anonymous craft + link share = free. **Email as channel** and **serious uploads** = pay (or hard prompt to pay).

## 1. Steps → time → cost → why they pay

Assumes organic visitor (SEO, webinar sample, partner link). Times are **median product-led**, not outbound SDR.

| Step | What they do | Time from land | Our $ cost* | Their feeling | Pay trigger |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| **1 · Land** | Open product / landing → “try without signup” | 0 | ~$0–2 (hosting) | Curious | — |
| **2 · First win** | Build a page **or** make 3 tasks **or** search names | **2–8 min** | ~$0.01–0.05 compute | “This works” | — |
| **3 · List** | Upload a small list (e.g. ≤200–500 rows) + tidy it | **10–25 min** | ~$0.05–0.20 | Effort invested | Soft: “Save / sync needs email” optional |
| **4 · Share link** | Copy link to colleague / client | **15–40 min** | ~$0 | Social proof inside firm | — |
| **5 · Email wall** | Try “email this” / send / put their address on the page | **Day 0–2** | ~$0.10–0.50 if we allow trial sends with ads | Professional risk (ads) or blocked | **Add email** → still Free w/ ads **or** pay Solo to look clean |
| **6 · File wall** | Attach files / re-upload big list / CRM import | **Day 0–7** | storage abuse risk | Blocked mid-job | **Pay Solo+** to unlock uploads beyond free list cap |
| **7 · Card** | Checkout Solo annual or Essentials annual (team) | **Same session or ≤72h** | payment fees ~3% | Commitment | Cash in |

\*Our variable cost per free user is tiny; **don’t** spend $1–2k CAC to acquire Free/Solo monthly.

### Time-to-paying (targets)

| Path | First useful moment | First **pay** moment | Day-one cash if they convert |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Solo (organic)** | ≤10 min (page/tasks) | **Day 0–3** when email-share or file upload hits | Solo annual **$108** (weak vs CAC — organic only) |
| **B · Team Essentials** | ≤25 min (list + share link) | **Day 1–7** when 2nd seat / no-ads / files | Essentials ×5 annual **~$2,100** |
| **C · Growth** | Demo or self-serve sequences need | **Day 3–14** | Growth ×3 annual **~$3,564** |
| **D · Enterprise** | Procurement after self-serve proof | **Week 2–8** | **$5k–$20k / yr** |

**Product-led KPI:** ≥40% of anonymous builders hit step 4 (link share) within 24h; ≥15% hit email or file wall within 7 days; **pay conversion from wall ≥8–12%** with founding offer.

## 2. Why they pay (psych in one line each)

1. **Ads on client-facing email/page** — identity threat (“looks cheap”).  
2. **Blocked mid-upload** — loss of progress after list endowment.  
3. **Colleague already on the shared link** — social proof / seat expansion.  
4. **Need to send from their domain** — can’t stay anonymous.  
5. **Founding clock** — one real window after first email add (not fake forever scarcity).

---

## 3. One-time offers (first sign-in / first email)

Show **once**, after they add email (or first sign-in), **only if** they haven’t paid. Prefer **term**, not monthly discount.

| Offer | When | Shape | Why it recovers cash |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Founding annual** | Within **72h** of first email / sign-in | **24 months for 14** on Essentials/Growth **team** carts (not Solo monthly) | Prepaid clears ~$1.5–2k CAC on 5-seat Essentials |
| **Launch setup** | Same window, optional bump | **$990** one-time (import + first page/campaign live) — 20–30% attach | Offsets CAC even if they pick Solo annual |
| **Clean Free→Solo** | At email-share or “remove ads” | Solo **annual** default ($9×12 = **$108**); monthly $19 is flexibility tax | Cheap unlock; **do not** buy traffic for this |
| **List unlock** | At file-upload wall | “Unlock uploads + ads off” → Solo annual CTA; if ≥3 invites detected → Essentials annual | Moves ICP up |
| **Pilot credit** | Sales-assisted only | **$990** credited to annual Essentials/Growth | Filters tire-kickers |

**Rules**

- Never discount **monthly** to close a paid-CAC lead.  
- Founding is **real cap** (e.g. first 50–100 *team* workspaces) + **72h** expiry in billing.  
- Anonymous users never see founding until they add email (avoids tire-kickers burning the offer).

## 4. Suggested free caps (so Free stays cheap)

| Surface | Free anonymous | After email (Free account) | Paid Solo+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tasks | Unlimited light / fair use | Same | Same + team |
| Pages | 1 public page | 1–3 | Plan limits |
| Name search | Soft rate limit | Higher | Full |
| List upload | **1 list · ≤500 rows · no file attachments** | Same + save to account | Larger / repeat / files |
| Link share | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Email share / send | Blocked or “add email” then **ads on** every send | Ads on | Ads off + higher limits |
| File attachments / big CSV | **Paywall** | **Paywall** | Unlocked |

## 5. One picture of the pipe

```
Land (no signup)
  → Tasks / Page / Name search          [$0, 2–8 min]
  → Upload list + link share            [$0, 10–40 min]  ← endowment
  → Want email share OR attach files
        ├─ Add email → Free w/ ads      (still $0)
        └─ Pay Solo annual / Essentials annual / founding
              → Day-one cash $108 / ~$2.1k / founding
```

**Bootstrap:** spend paid CAC only on paths that land **Essentials+ annual** or **Enterprise**. Use this freemium pipe to *feed* those, not to buy Solo monthly.
