# Close.com pricing base (captured 23 Aug 2026)

Source: [https://close.com/pricing](https://close.com/pricing) — verified live same day.
Currency: **USD**. Billing: per user / month. Annual saves up to ~50% on Solo.

## Headline plans

| Plan | Monthly / user | Annual / user (eff.) | Who |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Solo** | $19 | $9 | Founders / solo reps. **1 user max**, 10k leads, no workflows |
| **Essentials** | $49 | $35 | Small teams. Unlimited leads. Team collab. **No workflows, no Power Dialer, no bulk email** |
| **Growth** | $109 | $99 | Automation. Workflows, Power Dialer, custom activities, **bulk email**. Most popular |
| **Scale** | $149 | $139 | High-volume orgs. Roles, lead visibility, Predictive Dialer, unlimited recording retention, live coaching |

Custom / enterprise: talk to sales.

## What Close meters on top of seats

- **Calling + SMS**: usage passthrough (~$0.02/min outbound typical; numbers from ~$1/mo)
- **AI credits (Chloe)**: included pool per plan (500 / 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000), then buy more
- **Add-ons**: Premium numbers $19/line, Call Assistant $50/org + $0.02/min, extra orgs $50/mo

## Why this is the base for Forwardslash

1. Buyers already understand **per-user CRM + calling** pricing.
2. The jump from Solo → Essentials → Growth is the real commercial story (automation and dialling unlock).
3. Close has **no Free forever** — that is the open flank.
4. Close Solo annual at $9 is the parity floor for “normal” paid entry — we match Solo stickers ($19 / $9), and win on Free (ads + no uploads) plus enterprise bands.
