# Bootstrap unit economics — get $1–2k CAC back fast

**Constraint (Michael, 23 Aug 2026):** Bootstrapped. No investment float.  
Funded SaaS can buy customers at $1–2k for a $20/mo seat and wait. We cannot.  
Every acquisition channel in practice lands ~**$1,000–$2,000 CAC**. The commercial job is **day-one / ≤3 month cash recovery** from the buyer, then LTV.

Close list prices stay as the **sticker base**. The money model is what we put *around* those stickers (who we sell, term, offer, journey).

---

## 1. Channel cost references (2026 web + operator reality)

Your gut ($1–2k per paying workspace) matches fully-loaded B2B SaaS reality. Cheap “average CAC $300–400” numbers usually omit SDR salary, tools, and manager time.

### Labour / outbound (Upwork & remote)

| Input | Typical 2026 cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cold caller / appointment setter (Upwork) | **$8–20/hr** offshore · **$25–60/hr** stronger | Consistency beats cheap hours |
| Freelance / fractional SDR | **$1.5–4k/mo** part-time · **$25–60/hr** | Often split across clients |
| Nearshore full-time SDR | **~$1.4–4.5k/mo** all-in | Tools extra |
| US SDR fully loaded | **$5.5–9k/mo** | Not bootstrap-friendly |
| Outsourced SDR agency | **$5–10k/mo per rep** | |
| Tools stack (engagement + data + dialler + LinkedIn) | **$300–700/mo per rep** | Apollo/Clay/Sales Nav/Aircall-class |

**Back-of-envelope outbound:** 1 part-time setter @ $2k/mo + $400 tools = $2.4k.  
If they book 8 meetings/mo and 25% close → **2 customers → ~$1,200 CAC** before your time.  
Your time on demos/close easily pushes it to **$1.5–2.5k**. Same ballpark.

### Meetings cost (sales guy time)

| Step | Cost if you do it | Cost if hired |
|---|---|---|
| 20–30 min discovery | Your hour @ opportunity cost | $50–150 (contractor) |
| Demo + follow-ups (2–3 touches) | 1.5–3 hrs | Same |
| Close / procurement | Variable | — |

At 3:1 meeting→close, each closed deal carries **~3 meetings of labour**. That alone is hundreds of dollars even when “free.”

### Paid / content / partners (published 2025–26 ranges)

| Channel | Published CAC / CPL style figures | Bootstrap read |
|---|---|---|
| Partner / referral | ~$150–500 CAC (benchmarks); often **$500–3k** fully tagged | Cheapest *real* scale — still needs partner margin |
| Inbound / thought-leadership SEO | ~$650–1.8k CAC; CPL organic often lowest | Slow (6–18 mo). Good later, not day-1 cash machine |
| Content marketing (general) | ~$1.2k CAC class | Same |
| Paid search (B2B SaaS) | ~$800–3k+ CAC; SQL often £/$150–400+ | Fast but eats cash |
| LinkedIn ads | CPL ~$150–400; CAC often **$1–5k+** to closed | Dangerous on bootstrap unless ACV high |
| Events | ~$500 CAC channel avg; can be much higher | Rarely first lever |
| Fully-loaded SDR outbound | Often **$2–8k** in analyst ranges; First Page Sage-style outbound ~**$2k** | Matches your $1–2k experience when you count everything |

**Sources (directional, not gospel):** Optifai channel CAC, Miniloop outbound CAC, Upraw channel CAC, Repco meeting CAC, First Page Sage / MediaFast CAC-by-channel, GrowthSpree LinkedIn CPL, Upwork cold caller rate cards, remote SDR cost guides 2026.

### The bootstrap rule of thumb

| Motion | Looks cheap on a blog | What you actually pay to a *paying* customer |
|---|---|---|
| Upwork email + calls | $15/hr | **$1–2k** after conversion |
| “Just SEO posting” | Free time | **$1–2k** in months of work / freelance content |
| Partners who resell | Free leads | **$1–2k** via margin / co-marketing / your enablement |
| Ads | “$50 CPL” | **$1–2k** to closed-won |

**Plan as if every channel is $1–2k.** If a channel comes in under $800, treat it as luck, not a model.

## 5. Journey → money (moments that extract prepaid)

### Journey A — Organic Free (no CAC)

Aware (SEO/content) → Free (ads, no upload) → hits upload wall or ad fatigue → Solo annual (organic) or invites team → Essentials annual.

**Psych triggers:** effort already invested (contacts typed by hand), ad shame on client-facing email, “remove ads + unlock upload” at the exact blocked action.

### Journey B — Webinar → card trial → founding (paid CAC OK if team)

Webinar (“compliant outbound in 45 min”) → Business/Essentials **trial with card** → activation in 72h (list uploaded, 10 sends/dials, booking live) → **founding offer once** at activation → Launch setup bump.

**Psych triggers:**
- **Card early** = commitment + filters tire-kickers (continuity story: numbers/domains stay live).
- **Activation before discount** = they feel the product, then pay (endowment).
- **One real founding window** (72h + first N workspaces) = urgency without fake scarcity.
- **Annual default** in checkout; monthly is the “flexibility tax,” not the hero CTA.

### Journey C — Outbound / Upwork setter → demo → paid pilot / annual

List → multi-touch → meeting → demo → **paid pilot $990 credited to annual** *or* straight Essentials/Growth annual for 3–5 seats.

**Psych triggers:** renewal pain vs Close/HubSpot/dialler stack; seat-tax comparison only as *narrative*, bill is still per-user Close-parity; pilot fee kills tire-kickers and funds CAC.

### Journey D — Partner / agency channel

Partner runs client on Growth → client pays annual → partner takes **20–30% year-one**.  
Your net CAC often still lands $1–2k equivalent in margin — same rule: **only deals whose prepaid cash clears it**.

## 6. Offer ladder (bootstrap cash machine)

Aligned with Close stickers; designed for recovery:

1. **Founding (once, at activation):** 24 months for 14 on Essentials/Growth team carts · real cap · real 72h expiry in billing.  
2. **Launch / setup bump:** $990 one-time (import, domains, first campaign live). 20–30% attach → $200–300 CAC offset.  
3. **Annual (2 months free):** Close-like annual already embeds discount; keep it.  
4. **Quarterly (−5%):** cash-flow objection only.  
5. **Monthly full price:** allowed, but **not** a paid-acquisition target; no discount.  
6. **Free waiting room:** ads on · uploads off · nurture · next webinar.

**Rule:** never discount monthly to close a deal you bought for $1–2k. Change **term** or **seat count**, not the monthly rate.
