Questions

Plain answers.

Is Business really unlimited users?

Yes: ordinary collaborators can view, edit, comment, approve and work in the shared workspace. Only named people using bulk campaign or calling acceleration need an additional licence.

What happens when the free workspace grows?

Your data remains. You can upgrade the workspace, request company payment and transfer billing without rebuilding the system.

Does a calling licence include telephone minutes?

No. It pays for the calling workflow and productivity software. Number rental, call minutes, recording and transcription are provider costs shown separately.

Can we export everything?

The design principle is simple: access to your own records and a practical export are not premium hostage features.
