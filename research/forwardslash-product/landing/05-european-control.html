Five commitments

01 · PURPOSE

Collect with a reason

Make data source, intended use and visibility understandable rather than hidden in settings.

Trust requires specificity

What we will not pretend.

NO FALSE UNLIMITED

Resources have costs.

Phone minutes, purchased data and model usage will always have understandable allowances or prices.

NO SILENT AUTOMATION

Important action remains visible.

A draft is not a send. A suggested merge is not a merge. A prediction is not a fact.

NO PAPER COMPLIANCE

Claims follow capability.

Residency, certification and legal statements will be published only when operationally supportable.

Questions

Clear before commitment.

Is forwardslash.ch claiming full EU compliance today?

No. This page states the product and operating principles. Exact hosting, subprocessor, residency and certification claims must be published only when verified for the production service.

Why is Business sold in AUD on a European page?

The current launch and billing proposal is Australia-first while designing for European expectations. Local currency and tax handling should follow as the European offer becomes operational.

Can we use our own email or infrastructure provider?

Own-mailbox sending is central to Free, and advanced BYO provider/database options are part of the product direction where they can be supported responsibly.

Can a person start without company approval?

Yes. They can begin in a small free workspace, share a bounded result, then transfer billing and governance when the company is ready.
