Questions

Before you upload.

Will the import immediately send email?

No. Import, audience selection, drafting and sending are separate actions. A consequential send remains previewable and controllable.

Can I undo duplicate merges?

The intended workflow preserves a merge history and makes uncertain matches explicit before records are combined.

Why is research not unlimited?

Verification and enrichment are purchased from external providers. Showing their true cost protects you from a surprise bill and discourages wasteful jobs.

Can I just keep using the free list?

Yes. Free is a durable personal workspace, not a trial that deletes your work.
