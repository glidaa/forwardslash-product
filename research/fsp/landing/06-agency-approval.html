Questions

Before the first client campaign.

Does the client need an account?

A secure approval link can support bounded review. A full collaborator account is available when the client needs ongoing work visibility.

Can the client see another client’s data?

No. The intended agency model uses strict workspace and permission boundaries rather than filters that merely hide unrelated records.

Who pays for email and research usage?

The agency owns the software bill and can allocate visible provider usage to a client or campaign budget.

Can approval be bypassed?

Workspace policy determines which campaign types, audience sizes or senders require approval. The audit trail records the decision.
