From list to next action.

Build the queue

Choose a saved list, campaign replies, overdue follow-ups or opportunity stage. Exclude suppressed, wrong-region and recently contacted people.

Read the context

See company, role, relationship history, recent email, local time, call reason and the outcome you seek.

Click to call or use a controlled power queue. Use an approved number, recording policy and regional limits.

Connected, voicemail, referred, wrong person, call later, meeting booked or do not contact—with notes where needed.

Questions

Before you call.

Why are minutes not included?

Carrier prices differ by destination, number type and recording choice. Showing real usage prevents one operator’s calling pattern from being subsidised invisibly by every customer.

Can one licence be shared?

It is a named operator licence for accountability. Administrators can reassign it when responsibilities change.

Does forwardslash.ch decide whether recording is legal?

No. The product can enforce configured consent and retention policies, but the organisation remains responsible for its legal basis and regional requirements.

Can callers send a follow-up email?

Yes. The call outcome can create a draft, task or booking link in the same contact history.
