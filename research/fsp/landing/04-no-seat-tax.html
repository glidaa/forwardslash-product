Questions from finance

No hidden definition of unlimited.

What is an “ordinary collaborator”?

Someone viewing or editing records, handling projects and tasks, sending normal email, approving work, reporting or administering the workspace.

Can operator licences move between people?

The intended model allows administrators to reassign a named licence while preserving the previous operator’s activity history.

Why separate usage from licences?

A licence pays for workflow software and acceleration. A telephone carrier or data provider charges for actual consumption. Separating them makes both the value and the risk legible.

Can an individual pay and expense it?

Yes. The adoption design includes receipts, a company-payment request and a clean billing-owner transfer when procurement catches up.
